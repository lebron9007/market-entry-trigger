How this works▾

Each indicator below is evaluated independently against a fixed rule. When all five conditions are met simultaneously — a rare event — the historical base rate for positive 12-month forward returns in the S&P 500 is approximately 80–85%, based on retrospective analysis of major historical alignments (e.g. 2008–2009, March 2020).

This dashboard surfaces the framework's current state. It does not make recommendations and is not investment advice.

The five conditions▾

Each indicator's rule and known limitations.

1. VIX > 30

Trigger when CBOE VIX closes above 30.

Limitation: A daily spike can occur for idiosyncratic reasons (single-stock events, earnings season). A sustained reading carries more signal than a one-day move.

2. No hike at the next FOMC meeting

Trigger unless fed funds futures price a rate hike as more likely than not (≥50%) at the next FOMC meeting, using the CME FedWatch method. A hold or a cut meets the condition.

Limitation: Market pricing, not a Fed commitment, and it can swing quickly on data releases. Looks only at the next meeting. Computed from futures quotes via Yahoo, so figures can differ slightly from CME's own FedWatch page. Falls back to the FOMC dot plot if futures data is unavailable.

3. Margin debt decreasing

Trigger when FINRA monthly debit balances are lower than the prior month. A rise of under 1% shows as Watch.

Limitation: Lagging indicator — FINRA publishes ~3 weeks after month-end. Monthly granularity misses intra-month deleveraging.

4. Clear leading sector

Trigger when a growth sector ETF (Tech, Semis, Consumer Disc., Communications, Financials, Industrials) clearly outperforms the S&P 500 — a 3-month relative strength of at least 3pp. The Earnings condition then confirms those outperformers are beating EPS.

Limitation: 3-month window; momentum can reverse quickly. Defensive sectors (Staples, Utilities, Health Care, Real Estate) and commodity sectors are excluded because they beat a falling market by default. Identifies that capital is flowing somewhere — does not validate the underlying thesis.

5. Leading sector earnings

Trigger when ≥60% of the leading sector's top-10 constituents beat their consensus EPS estimate in the most recent quarter.

Limitation: Quarterly cadence; constituents don't all report in the same quarter. Constituents with no data are excluded, and the condition only passes if confirmed beats alone reach 60%. Backward-looking — beating last quarter does not predict the next.
